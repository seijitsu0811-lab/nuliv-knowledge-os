## Ingestion Decision

This source is suitable for NuLiv Knowledge OS because it supports:

- Human-centered assessment: sleep is connected to energy, emotion, cognition,
  immunity, metabolism, family concern, and daily function.
- Trust-building: sleep can be explained through clear data, questionnaires,
  before/after tracking, and wearable/HRV feedback.
- Closed-loop care: evaluation, sleep behavior, nutrition, activity,
  environment, medical referral, and follow-up can be iterated.
- Internal training: staff can learn how to explain sleep without reducing it to
  sleeping hours only.

## Fixed QA Suggestions

### Q1. 喜悅為什麼要重視睡眠？

Sleep influences immunity, metabolism, cardiovascular risk, cognition, emotion,
and daily recovery. For NuLiv, sleep is not just a symptom; it is a core part of
understanding whether a person has enough recovery capacity.

### Q2. 糖尿病個案為什麼要看睡眠？

The source report links sleep deprivation with appetite hormones, BMI, glucose
tolerance, diabetes risk, OSA, HRV, and autonomic regulation. For a diabetes
case, NuLiv should ask about sleep duration, sleep quality, snoring, daytime
sleepiness, stress, and recovery.

### Q3. 什麼情況要懷疑 OSA？

Snoring, daytime sleepiness, obesity, older age, male sex, hypertension,
diabetes risk, cardiovascular risk, and observed breathing pauses during sleep
all raise concern. AHI and PSG/CPC-style screening can help decide next steps.

### Q4. 第一線可以先做什麼睡眠衛教？

Start with stable sleep/wake time, caffeine and alcohol boundaries, screen/light
management, exercise timing, sleep environment, side sleeping when appropriate,
and stimulus control. If symptoms are persistent or suggest OSA, refer for
medical evaluation.

### Q5. 哪些內容可以給 AI 當正式回答依據？

Items marked `醫學文獻` and `符合` can be used more confidently. Items marked
`內部觀察` or `需修正` should be used only as internal service ideas and must not
be presented as medical conclusions.
